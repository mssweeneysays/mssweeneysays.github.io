Your dates

One deadline. No early round at UC Berkeley.

  1. Nov 30UC application due, including your 4 PIQs. One application covers every UC campus.
  2. After you applyBerkeley emails some applicants an invite to send up to 2 letters of recommendation. Check your email.
  3. Mar 2Financial aid priority date (FAFSA or California Dream Act).
  4. By Mar 31Decisions out.
  5. May 1Deadline to say yes.

UC dates and deadlines (admission.universityofcalifornia.edu), checked Nov 6, 2026. Letters: Berkeley Admissions, Letters of Recommendation at UC Berkeley. Decision and reply dates: Sections C16–C17, 2025–26 Common Data Set.

What I saw as an admissions reader

When 9 out of 10 first-years have a 3.75 or higher, your grades get you read. They don't get you picked. Your PIQs are where the reader finds a reason to fight for you. Give them one real story per question.

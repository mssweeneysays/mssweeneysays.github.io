What NYU actually cares about from the data

5 things are top-tier. Two of them are about who you are, not what you did.

Your recommendation letters and your character sit right next to your grades. Activities are only "considered." So the people who write about you, and how your essay sounds, matter a lot here.

What I saw as an admissions reader

At NYU, your letters and your character are rated as high as your grades. So your essay and your recommender should describe the same person. Pick one quality you want a reader to remember, and make sure both show it.

— Ms Sweeney

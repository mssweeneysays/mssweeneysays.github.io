Decide this first

Your Berkeley major is all or nothing for business

  • Berkeley, Business Administration (Haas): it has to be your first-choice major. Haas doesn't review it as an alternate. If Haas doesn't admit you, you aren't admitted to Berkeley at all. from the data
  • Haas has its own extra application: a short essay and a recorded video interview. It opens after you submit the UC application and is due in early January. If it's missing, the application is denied.
  • Berkeley, Global Management Program: the exception. If you're not admitted to it, Berkeley still considers you as undeclared in Letters & Science.
  • UCLA: simpler. The College doesn't use your major in its review, and business students start as pre-majors (for example, Pre-Business Economics) and apply to the major later.
What it means for youYour PIQs work for any of these paths, so this is your family's call. Just choose it on purpose before you hit submit.

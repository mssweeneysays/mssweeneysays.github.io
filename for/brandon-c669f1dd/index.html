What all 4 campuses agree on

Your Econ prep is the main event

For transfer students, every one of your campuses starts with the same question: have you finished the lower-division Economics courses, with strong grades, by spring? from the data

Your PIQs explain the person behind those grades. The rest of your application should back up both.

Calculus is the piece to double-check. Every campus asks for a series, not one class.

UC Berkeley

Berkeley Economics

  • New Starting fall 2027, transfer students who pick Economics on the application are declared in the major automatically. from the data
  • The department asks students to do everything they can to finish the Math 1A/1B calculus series for the major's prerequisites.
What it means for youPick Economics on your application, and make sure your calculus series matches Berkeley's on ASSIST.

UCLA

UCLA Economics

  • Prep: 1 microeconomics course, 1 macroeconomics course and 1 year of calculus, done by spring. UCLA strongly recommends finishing by fall. from the data
  • You have to be admitted straight into Economics (as Pre-Economics). You can't switch into it after you enroll.
  • UCLA lists working while you're in school as one of its personal criteria.
What it means for youApply to UCLA as Economics, not something else, and make your restaurant job easy to see in your activities.
UCLA cheat sheet →
UC Santa Barbara

UCSB Economics

  • Economics starts as a pre-major. You get into the major by earning a 2.75 GPA or better across 4 prep courses: macroeconomics, microeconomics and 2 calculus courses. from the data
  • Community college transfer centers suggest choosing an alternate major outside Economics for the best chance at UCSB.
What it means for youChoose an alternate major you'd actually be happy in.
UCSB cheat sheet →
UC Davis

UC Davis Economics

  • Economics is a selective major, with extra course and GPA requirements beyond regular admission. from the data
  • Prep courses have to be taken for a letter grade, with no grade below a C, and finished by the end of spring.
  • Davis needs at least a 2.80 GPA for admission, and selective majors ask for more.
What it means for youCheck DVC's Economics agreement with Davis on ASSIST, and keep every prep grade a C or better.

Your 3 majors

Pick each major by its exact name

  • UC Santa Barbara, Linguistics with a Speech-Language Sciences and Disorders emphasis: built to give students the courses they need for graduate programs in speech-language pathology. UCSB says it's the only UC with an undergraduate major in this area. from the data
  • UC San Diego, Linguistics: Speech and Language Sciences: made for students considering speech-language pathology. UCSD says it isn't the same as a Communication Sciences and Disorders degree, so some master's programs may ask for extra courses. The department partners with local state schools so students can take them. from the data
  • UC Davis, Linguistics: a general linguistics major. It doesn't have a speech track.
What it means for youUCSB lines up most directly with speech-language pathology, and UCSD is close. In the UC app, choose UCSD's major as "Linguistics: Speech and Language Sciences." For UCSB, pick the Speech-Language Sciences and Disorders emphasis if the app lists it; if it only shows "Linguistics," choose that and ask a UCSB linguistics advisor about the emphasis once you're admitted.

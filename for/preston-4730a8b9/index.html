UCLA

UCLA Mathematics of Computation

  • Every UCLA math major needs about 1.5 years of calculus through multivariable, plus Linear Algebra or Differential Equations. UCLA strongly recommends both, plus 1 C++ course. from the data
  • Transfers start as pre-majors until the lower-division prep is finished at UCLA.
  • UCLA gives first priority to California community college and UC students. Students from 4-year schools like USD compete for the remaining spots, so finished prep and strong PIQs count even more.
What it means for youFinish Linear Algebra, Differential Equations and C++ by spring. They're exactly the courses UCLA asks for.

UC San Diego

UCSD Applied Math and Probability & Statistics

  • All UCSD math majors, including both of yours, screen transfers on the same courses: Linear Algebra and 3 quarters of calculus. Differential Equations is recommended. from the data
  • Each one needs a C or better, finished by the end of spring. UCSD looks at how many prep courses you finished, your grades in them and your overall GPA.
  • UCSD only has course agreements with California community colleges, so your USD courses get checked one by one.
What it means for youKeep every math grade strong this year, and write your USD course titles in the application exactly as they appear on your transcript.

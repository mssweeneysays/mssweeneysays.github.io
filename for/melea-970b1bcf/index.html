Your majors

Your family's call, and here's what I checked

Political science and public affairs fit your record. Your environmental justice work is policy work too, so it strengthens your case instead of pulling you toward a different major.

  • Berkeley: first-years are admitted to the College of Letters & Science and declare Political Science after taking intro political science courses at Berkeley. from the data
  • UCLA: choose Public Affairs as your pre-major on the application. Pre-majors apply to the major itself by winter quarter of their second year.
  • UC Davis: choose Political Science.
  • UC Santa Barbara: you'd start as a Pre-Political Science major. The full major comes after your lower-division political science courses and a minimum GPA in them. UCSB says pre-major status doesn't make admission harder.
One change in your PIQsAll 4 campuses read the same 4 PIQs. The last lines of PIQ 4 name Political Science, so word them to fit UCLA's Public Affairs too (see the comment in your doc).

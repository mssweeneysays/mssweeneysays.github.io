Your major

Your family's call, and here's what I checked

Your doc lists Biology, your brief lists Public Health at Berkeley and Human Biology & Society at UCLA, and your PIQ 6 points to public health. Readers see your major right next to your PIQs, so pick one per campus and make them match.

  • Berkeley: Public Health is a capped (high-demand) major, so Berkeley asks students to plan an alternate major. Students who choose it on the application and are admitted can be guaranteed a spot once they finish the prerequisites. from the data
  • UCLA: first-years can't enter Public Health directly; they start as pre-majors. You can choose pre-Human Biology & Society when you apply, but the major itself takes a later application and is highly selective.
  • UC San Diego, Davis, Irvine: pick a major for each before you submit.
If you choose…What changes in your PIQs
Public Health / Human Biology & SocietyPIQ 6 already fits. Name the exact major at the end. Choose an alternate major for Berkeley. Update the "Intended major" line at the top of your doc.
BiologyPIQ 6's last lines change: name the science you want to study and lean on AP Bio, your research method and data. Your PIQ 6 classroom move becomes more important, and PIQ 5's AP Bio line matters more too.

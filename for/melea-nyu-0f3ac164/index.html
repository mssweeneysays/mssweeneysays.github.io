The short version

  1. NYU uses the same Common App you'll fill out anyway. Melea already has most of what she needs: her essay draft, her activities and the stories from her UC essays.
  2. At NYU, essays, recommendation letters and character count as much as grades. Activities count less. So the next 6 weeks are about her writing and the people who write about her.
  3. 3 decisions come first, by Sat Dec 5: which round (Early Decision II is binding), which program, and who writes her letter. The steps below are in order of importance.

Line it up

3 places your essay and activities must match

A reader sees your essay and your Activities list side by side. If they tell the same story in different ways, the reader stops and wonders.

  • HBCU Club. Your essay says your freshman school shut it down. Your Activities entry should say where it started, how big it got and what happened after, in the same grades and numbers.
  • Varsity volleyball. Your essay says when you got called up. Make the grades in your volleyball entry match it.
  • Your art. Your essay is built on how you make art. Black Creatives Club and Ryman Arts back it up, so put them in your top 10, not in honors only.
You and KarisYou're both applying to NYU, so the same office may read both of your files. Keep your stories your own: your Jack and Jill entry leads with your 4 elected offices and the projects you led, and your essays don't share moments with hers.

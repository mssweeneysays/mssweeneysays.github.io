What UC Santa Barbara actually cares about from the data

Only 2 things are top-tier: your GPA and your PIQs.

Your GPA is mostly set by senior fall. Your essays, which UC calls Personal Insight Questions (PIQs), are the other half of UCSB's top tier, and you can still change them before Nov 30. Activities are only "considered" here.

Your dates

One deadline. No early round at UCSB.

  1. Nov 30UC application due, including your 4 PIQs. One application covers every UC campus.
  2. Dec 1–31Window to ask UCSB to change the major on your application.
  3. Jan 5College of Creative Studies priority deadline for its separate application (CCS applicants only).
  4. Mar 2Financial aid priority date (FAFSA or California Dream Act).
  5. By Mar 31Decisions out.
  6. May 1Deadline to say yes.

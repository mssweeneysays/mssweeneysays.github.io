What UC does, month by month

For fall 2027 applicants. Every date is from UC's own pages.

  1. Dec
    Your file goes to every campus you picked.

    Each campus reviews it on its own. Each one emails to confirm it got your application.

  2. Dec
    A random group gets a verification email.

    UC asks them to prove one thing on the application: an award, an activity, a job, even a PIQ detail. Miss the deadline in that email, and the application is withdrawn at every UC campus.

    random = not in trouble. Just send proof on time.
  3. Oct to Dec
    Berkeley invites some first-year applicants to send letters of recommendation.

    Up to 2 letters, at least 1 from a teacher in a core academic subject. The due date is in the invite.

  4. Dec to Jan
    Reading season.

    Trained readers score your whole file. At UCLA, every application is read twice, start to finish.

  5. By Jan
    Every campus sends a portal login.

    That portal is where your decision shows up. Set it up the day the email lands.

  6. Jan 31
    Transfer Academic Update due. Transfers

    Required for every transfer applicant, even with nothing to change.

  7. Mar 2
    Money deadline.

    FAFSA or California Dream Act Application, plus the Cal Grant GPA Verification Form.

  8. March
    First-year decisions post.

    Some campuses release earlier. Transfer decisions come by the end of April.

  9. May 1
    Say yes to one campus.

    Submit your Statement of Intent to Register. Transfers have until June 1.

  10. Jul 1
    Final transcripts due to the campus you're attending.

    Your offer is provisional until they check it.

If you only do one thing, and avoid one thing

#1 Do

Treat your inbox and portals like part of the application.

The verification email, Berkeley's letter invite and the portal logins all come by email. Missing one is the only way a finished application can still fall apart.

The verification email, Berkeley's letter invite and the portal logins all come to your student's email. Offer to be the second set of eyes: a quick Sunday check together through January.

#1 Don't

Don't let senior grades slip, or quietly drop a class.

Every UC offer is provisional. Campuses check your final transcript, and a D or F in senior year can get an offer pulled.

Every UC offer is provisional until the final transcript arrives. A D or F in senior year can get an offer pulled. Ask to see grades at each progress report, not just in June.
